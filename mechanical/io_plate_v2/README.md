## Geometry (mm)

- **Outline:** 51.9 × 163.1, R 11.5, centred at (53.19, 77.07). This fits the stock plate scan (straight width 51.89, Y −4.47…158.62).
- **Body:** 1.2 mm skin; outer face a cylinder of R 82 (`CASE_R`) about the Y axis. Perimeter rim 1.2 wide × 3.0 deep.
- **USB-C ×6:** 9.6 × 4.0, R 1.8 (≈0.33 mm per side around an ≈8.94 × 3.26 shell), at X 42.6 / 63.9 and Y 75.2 / 65.45 / 55.7.
  - The receptacle shell comes up through the skin so the mouth is flush with its flat land (see Port angle handling).
  - A USB-C plug shell is about 6.65 mm long, so the mouth must sit within about 0.6 mm of the land.
- **USB-A ×4:** 13.6 × 6.0. **RJ45:** 13.0 × 10.7. **HDMI:** 15.4 × 6.0.
  - Each sits on its flat land and has a 1.0 mm-wall collar, 1.0 mm long, behind the land.
  - These connectors tolerate setback.
- **AC inlet:** stock window, 34.55 × 24.65, R 3.
- **Audio holes:** Ø4.8 at the stock positions.
- **Power button:** Ø12.4 at (63.02, 108.03). It takes a separately printed clear cap; the power/sleep LED D20 sits under it.
- **Light pipes:** Ø2.2 bores (for Ø2.0 PMMA rods) with Ø4.4 × 2 mm bosses at the six icon LEDs D21–D26.
- **Frame centre screw:** Ø6 × 0.6 relief at (53.38, 58.38) for the screw head. Check the head height (M-IOF2).
- **Snap clips (VERIFY):** at the stock clip points (31.0, 140.0), (28.2, 90.0), (29.6, 30.3) and (44.1, 2.7), plus their mirror images about X 53.19.
  - Tabs are 1.2 thick × 5.0 wide × 6.0 long with a 0.5 mm hook (MJF PA12, about 2.5 % strain).

## Port angle handling (curved plate + flat lands; plan §4.4)

- The stock ports fan outward about **7° per column** (±1.5°, mirror-symmetric). Sources: Aidan's edge-on photo (7.3° / 6.4°) and the case cylinder geometry (asin(10.6/82) = 7.4°).
- The plate's outer face is a cylinder, `CASE_R` = 82 (M-IOT2; `None` = flat).
- Each port group sits on a **flat land parallel to the board**, recessed into the curve (outboard edge flush). Behind each land is a 1.0 mm boss that sits inside the frame slot. All new connectors are straight vertical parts on a flat board.

| Land | z below crown | Inboard pocket | Boss | Frame slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LAND_C_H / _O (USB-C ×3) | −1.55 / −1.58 | 1.38 / 1.40 | 11.8 × 25.7 | 12.2 × 30.2 |
| LAND_A_H / _O (USB-A ×2) | −1.93 / −2.15 | 1.90 / 2.1 | 16.8 × 19.3 | 17.6 × 20.0 |
| LAND_ETH1 | −1.87 | – | 15.4 × 13.1 | 16.7 wide |
| LAND_HDMI | −2.25 | – | 17.8 × 8.4 | 18.3 × 9.0 |

- USB-C mouths are flush with the land. USB-A, RJ45 and HDMI faces sit 1.2 mm behind the land.
- Required connector height = D0 (board-to-crown, M-IOF2) − depth from `io_plate_v2_A0_features.json`.
- A residual board-to-plate tilt measured in M-IOT1 goes into `TILT_AX_DEG` / `TILT_AY_DEG` (pivot `TILT_REF`). Rebuild with `/workspace/cadenv/bin/python build_plate.py [--eth2 open]`.

## Process, material, tolerances (ordering at JLC3DP or PCBWay)

| Process | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MJF PA12, dyed black** | **Recommended (rev A)** | Tough, clips survive, no supports. About $10–20 each. |
| SLA black resin (tough/ABS-like) | Cosmetic second print | Best surface. Brittle clips: use L 7.0, hook 0.4. Supports go on the inner face. |
| CNC (ABS, PC, POM) | Final batch only | ±0.05, about $80–200 (curved face + pockets + clips). |
| Clear SLA, polished | Power-button cap | Light passes through to D20. Cap CAD is TODO (Ø12.0 × 3 on a Ø6 stem). |

**Design values:**
- Skin 1.2; rim 1.2 × 3.0; collars 1.0 wall; land backing 1.0; light-pipe boss wall 1.1; clip tab 1.2.
- MJF minimum wall is about 0.8 (1.0 recommended); SLA about 0.6–0.8 [vendor rules, check on the order page].
- Tolerance about ±0.2–0.3 mm or ±0.3 % (±0.5 over the 163 mm length). Openings carry ≥ 0.3 per side; USB-C is 9.6 × 4.0 R1.8 around an 8.94 × 3.26 shell.
- MJF holes print 0.1–0.2 small: light-pipe bores are Ø2.2 for Ø2.0 PMMA rods (ream if tight).
- **Snap clips:** 1.2 t × 5.0 w × 6.0 L, hook 0.5, so bending strain ≈ 1.5·t·y/L² = 2.5 % (PA12 OK). Too stiff: file the hook. Loose: hook 0.6.
- Plate-to-frame clearance is 0.2–0.3 per side.

**Order:**
- Upload `io_plate_v2_A0.stl` (or `.step`): MJF PA12, dyed black, qty 2, standard bead-blast finish, no supports.
- Rev-B Ethernet uses `io_plate_v2_A0_eth2open.*`.
- **Not ordered.** Measure M-IOT2 (curvature) and M-IOF2 (D0) first.
